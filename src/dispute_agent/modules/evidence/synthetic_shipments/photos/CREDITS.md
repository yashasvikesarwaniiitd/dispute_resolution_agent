# Parcel photo credits

Real photographs from Wikimedia Commons, used under their free licences, scaled to 960 px wide and otherwise unchanged. They stand in for the parcel photo a seller or hub would attach to a dispute. The shipments, sellers and cases they are attached to are synthetic (I11); no photo shows a real seller's parcel.

Fourteen are attached to the synthetic shipments (`modules/evidence/synthetic_shipments/photos/`). Five are used only by the image check's evaluation set (`modules/evals/parcel_photos/`).
